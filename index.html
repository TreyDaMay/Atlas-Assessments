Atlas Assessments is a developing academic assessment project designed to explore student achievement, identify learning needs, and make progress easier to understand.

Explore assessments

Choose an assessment category to get started.

S
Available

Standard Assessments

Explore questions aligned with the skills students are generally expected to learn at a selected grade level. Follow-up questions help investigate topics that may need more practice.

G
In development

Growth Assessments

A separate assessment concept intended to estimate a student's current academic level across different grades and track changes over time using a future calibrated scoring model.

About Atlas: This website is an independent prototype. Its current questions and results are for practice and experimentation. They are not official school assessments, and its scores are not standardized or validated measures of academic achievement.
